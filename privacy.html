PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

How this portfolio handles your information

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This page explains the contact options and third-party services used by this static portfolio.

Project enquiries

When you choose Send enquiry, the contact form sends the name, email address, service, project brief and any optional budget or timeline information you provided to FormSubmit, a third-party service that routes form submissions by email. Form submissions are not stored in a database operated by this GitHub Pages portfolio. FormSubmit states it may retain submissions for 30 days in its archive. See its published policies for details.

Email drafts

The Open email draft option opens your own email application. Nothing is sent by this website when that option is used. You decide whether to send the message.

Hosting and external resources

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads typefaces from Google Fonts. These services may receive basic request information such as your IP address. This portfolio does not currently install custom visitor analytics or advertising trackers.

What not to send

Please do not submit passwords, government identifiers, sensitive project records, or confidential information in the contact form.

Contact and removal requests

To discuss a previous enquiry or request removal of information in an email exchange, use the contact details on the contact page.
